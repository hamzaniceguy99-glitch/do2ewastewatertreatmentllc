How we work

A Florida supplier, clear about where our scope ends

DO2E Wastewater Treatment LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L07000087596, filed in 2007, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We supply and service equipment. We do not provide professional engineering design, we are not a licensed septic tank or plumbing contractor, and we do not prepare permit applications — those go to licensed engineers and contractors. Your discharge permit, monitoring and regulatory reporting remain yours, and we will not promise an effluent number that depends on how a plant is run.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will this get us back into permit compliance?

We cannot promise that, and anyone who does is guessing. Treatment performance depends on influent, loading, temperature and operation. We will tell you what the equipment can realistically do.

Can you avoid us having to dredge?

Often a biological programme recovers useful volume. Sometimes the sludge is too deep or too inorganic and dredging is the honest answer — we will say so after the survey.

Do you do the engineering drawings?

No. That requires a licensed professional engineer, and we will work alongside yours or refer you to one.

Do you handle permitting?

No. Permit applications and regulatory reporting stay with the operator and your engineer. We supply the data from our equipment that supports them.

How quickly can you get to a site?

We reply within 48 hours and schedule assessments by appointment. Service agreement customers get priority on breakdowns.

Who do you typically work with?

Municipal utilities, industrial plants with their own treatment, and agricultural operations with lagoons.

Program 02 · Capacity back, without dredging

Sludge reduction & biological treatment

Sludge accumulation quietly removes the capacity a lagoon was designed around. Biological reduction programmes can recover a good part of it at a fraction of the cost of dredging, and we are straightforward about the cases where dredging is genuinely the answer instead.

Odour & surface control

Odour complaints are what turn a technical problem into a political one. Most of it traces back to anaerobic conditions and poor surface mixing, so that is where we start rather than with a masking agent that buys a fortnight.

Program 04 · Before it fails

Servicing, parts & inspection

Aeration equipment runs continuously in a hostile environment, and the failures are predictable: bearings, diffusers, blowers, membranes. Scheduled inspection catches those while they are parts rather than emergencies, and we keep the common ones available.
